Instagram follow-back checker. Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

How to get your file

  1. Open Instagram, go to Settings, then Accounts Center.
  2. Tap Your information and permissions, then Download your information.
  3. Choose Followers and following only. Set format to JSON and date range to All time.
  4. Request the download. Instagram emails you when it is ready, from a few minutes to a few hours.
  5. Download the ZIP and drop it in the box above. Do not unzip it.

Repeat every week or two with a fresh ZIP. Each upload adds a snapshot, which unlocks the Unfollowed you tab and the history chart.
